Here are the complete trading strategy rules for the ADX indicator as explained in the video:

*   **Indicators & Settings:**
    *   **DMI (Directional Movement Index):** This is the primary indicator used, which includes the ADX line.
        *   **ADX Line (Black):** Represents the strength of the market momentum.
        *   **+DI Line (Green):** Represents buying momentum.
        *   **-DI Line (Red):** Represents selling momentum.
        *   *Note: The speaker uses default periods but changes the colors for clarity.*
    *   **Moving Average Ribbon (EMA):** Used as a secondary confirmation indicator.
        *   **Fast EMA:** 9-period (Set to Green).
        *   **Slow EMA:** 21-period (Set to Red).

*   **Timeframe:**
    *   The strategy is demonstrated on **5-minute** and **15-minute** charts, though the speaker mentions it can be adapted to your preference.

*   **Entry Conditions:**
    *   **Buy Entry:**
        1.  The **+DI (Green line)** must be moving upwards and cross above the **-DI (Red line)**, showing buyers are taking control.
        2.  The **ADX (Black line)** must start curving and pointing upwards, confirming that the upward momentum is strong and fast.
        3.  **Confirmation:** On the price chart, the **9 EMA (Green)** should cross above the **21 EMA (Red)**.
    *   **Sell Entry:**
        1.  The **-DI (Red line)** must be moving upwards and cross above the **+DI (Green line)**, showing sellers are taking control.
        2.  The **ADX (Black line)** must start curving and pointing upwards, confirming strong downward momentum.
        3.  **Confirmation:** On the price chart, the **9 EMA (Green)** should cross below the **21 EMA (Red)**.

*   **Stop Loss and Target:**
    *   **Target:** The speaker suggests aiming for a risk-to-reward ratio of **1:2** or **1:5**.
    *   **Stop Loss/Exit:** Use a **trailing stop loss** to ride the trend. Alternatively, you should exit the trade when the momentum shifts against you. For example, in a buy trade, exit when the -DI (Red line) starts rising and crosses back over the +DI (Green line), indicating sellers are returning.

*   **When to Avoid Trades:**
    *   **Do not enter** any trades when the **ADX (Black line) is pointing downwards**. A downward-sloping ADX indicates that the market momentum is slow or non-existent, making it a poor time for this specific strategy. Always wait for the ADX line to start moving up before considering an entry.
