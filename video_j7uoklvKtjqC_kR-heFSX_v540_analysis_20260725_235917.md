Based on a careful analysis of the video, here are the specific trading rules, quality filters, and criteria Dr. Devendra Pratap Singh outlines for the "Red Bar Theory" setup:

**1. Minimum GREEN bricks before a red bar:**
He does not specify a strict minimum numerical count of green bricks. However, he strongly emphasizes that the red bar must appear after a clear, established uptrend. He relies on visual momentum rather than a fixed count.
*   *Quote/Context:* At **14:26** and **17:21**, while circling large upward swings, he emphasizes the required momentum, saying, *"Dekhiye kitna strong up move hai"* (See how strong the up move is). The logic is that the market must show strong institutional buying before the red bar (profit booking) occurs.

**2. EMA Trend Cloud & Moving Averages:**
While his custom indicator ("Dr. Devendra Smart Renko Engine: PRO Edition") includes options for EMA 10, EMA 30, and an EMA Trend Cloud (visible in the settings at **02:46**), he explicitly disables them for this core strategy.
*   *Quote/Context:* At **03:00**, he unchecks these options to clear the chart, stating he is removing them to focus purely on the price action of the bricks for this specific concept.

**3. Skipping early morning setups (First 15-30 mins):**
Yes, he explicitly advises against taking the very first setups that form right after the market opens due to erratic price action.
*   *Exact Words:* At **13:34**, he states: *"Hamesha yaad rakhiye... subah subah 9:15 se lekar 9:30, 9:45 tak market mein volatility bahut jyada hoti hai... Toh koshish kariye... subah ka jo pehla trade milta hai... usko aap avoid kariye."* (Always remember... in the morning from 9:15 to 9:30, 9:45 there is a lot of volatility in the market... So try... the first trade you get in the morning... avoid it.)

**4. Trade frequency limits:**
Yes, he strongly advocates for limiting the number of trades to maintain discipline and protect capital.
*   *Exact Words:* At **18:26**, he instructs: *"Din mein sirf ek ya do trade lijiye."* (Take only one or two trades in a day). He warns that overtrading will ultimately lead to a net loss by the end of the day.

**5. Invalid Setups / When to SKIP a Red Bar:**
A setup is immediately invalidated if the price breaks below the low of the red bar before triggering the entry at the high.
*   *Exact Words/Context:* At **04:32**, he draws an 'X' below the red bar's low and explains: *"Agar red bar ka low break ho jata hai, toh setup invalid ho jayega."* (If the low of the red bar is broken, the setup becomes invalid).

**6. Entry Timing (Immediate cross vs. Candle Close):**
He strictly requires the green brick to **close** above the high of the red bar, not just wick or push past it momentarily.
*   *Exact Words:* At **04:40**, while pointing to the entry trigger line, he says: *"Jaise hi agla green bar is red bar ke high ke upar close karta hai..."* (As soon as the next green bar closes above the high of this red bar...).

**7. Higher Timeframe or SPOT chart usage:**
The entire analysis demonstrated in the video is performed directly on the **SPOT Index charts** (e.g., NIFTY 50 Index, S&P BSE Sensex Index). Because Renko charts are price-based rather than time-based, he does not mention checking higher timeframes (like 1H or Daily). The trend is dictated by the chosen brick size (e.g., 10 points for Nifty, 40 points for Sensex).

**8. Ranging/Sideways market avoidance:**
He explicitly warns against trading this setup in choppy or range-bound conditions, as the strategy relies on trend continuation.
*   *Exact Words:* At **18:40**, he advises: *"Agar market sideways hai, ek range mein fasa hua hai, toh wahan par koi bhi setup kaam nahi karega."* (If the market is sideways, stuck in a range, then no setup will work there).

**9. Red Bar SIZE filter:**
He does not mention avoiding "large" red bars. In the context of Renko charts, the size of a single brick is fixed by the user's settings (e.g., 10 points). Therefore, a single red brick will always be that exact size.

**10. Stop Loss Placement:**
The strict rule for risk management is placing the Stop Loss just below the low of the trigger red bar.
*   *Context:* At **04:35**, he writes "SL" directly below the bottom wick/edge of the red bar, indicating that if the price reverses and takes out the red bar's low after entry, the trade must be exited.
