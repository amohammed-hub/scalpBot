Based on the video provided, here are the complete trading strategy rules for using the Fixed Range Volume Profile:

**Chart Setup & Timeframe**
*   **Timeframe:** Use a 5-minute or 15-minute chart.
*   **Range Selection:** 
    *   **Method 1 (Daily):** Select the entire price range of the previous day before the current trading session begins.
    *   **Method 2 (Swing):** Draw the profile from a recent distinct swing high point to a swing low point.

**Understanding Key Levels**
*   **Value Area High (VAH):** The upper boundary of the value area, which acts as a resistance level.
*   **Value Area Low (VAL):** The lower boundary of the value area, which acts as a support level.
*   **Point of Control (POC) / Point of Interest (POI):** The price level with the highest traded volume. It acts as a "magnetic level" that attracts the price.

**Trading Rules & Entries**

**1. Support & Resistance Strategy (Reversals)**
The core rule for reversal trades depends on the location of the POC relative to the value area boundaries.
*   **Short Entry (Sell):** Wait for the price to approach the VAH and show signs of rejection. You should only initiate a short trade from the VAH if the **POC is located near the VAH**.
*   **Long Entry (Buy):** Wait for the price to approach the VAL and show signs of rejection. You should only initiate a long trade from the VAL if the **POC is located near the VAL**.
*   *Note:* If the price is at a boundary (e.g., VAH) but the POC is at the opposite end (e.g., near VAL), a reversal trade is not recommended from that boundary.

**2. Breakout Strategy**
While the video title mentions fake breakouts, the specific strategy demonstrated shows how to trade a price breaking back into the value area.
*   **Long Breakout Entry:** If the price has dropped below the value area, wait for it to break back *inside* the value area by crossing above the VAL. Enter a long trade upon this inward breakout.

**Stop Loss & Targets**
*   **Stop Loss:** Place your stop loss near the boundary level you used for entry. For example, in a long breakout above the VAL, place the stop loss just around or slightly below the VAL.
*   **Target:** Aim for a Risk/Reward ratio of 1:2 or 1:3. Alternatively, you can use a trailing stop loss to capture larger movements.
